## Submission Types

> **Presenters were required to attend in Seattle.**

### Short Talk
- **Duration:** 10–15 minutes  
- **Focus:** A package, an application note, or a current research presentation  
- **Notes:** Presentations seeking collaboration or input from the community were encouraged.

### Long Workshop
- **Duration:** 1.5–2 hours  
- **Format:** Interactive workshop  
- **Notes:** Participants were expected to follow along and perform analyses themselves.

### Posters
- **Format:** Physical poster  
- **Focus:** A package, research idea, or other relevant topics.

### Package Demos
- **Format:** Interactive demo during the poster session  
- **Details:**
  - Presenters had a dedicated table
  - Presenters used their own laptop to demonstrate the package
  - Opportunity to answer questions and discuss design decisions  
- **Intent:** A drop-in, interactive experience rather than a scheduled talk.
